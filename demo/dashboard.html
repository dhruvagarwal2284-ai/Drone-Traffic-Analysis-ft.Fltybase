Interactions between road users

the gap nothing in the field measures

A perspective view of one approach cannot place two road users in a common coordinate frame, so conflicts go unmeasured. With trajectories in metres both standard surrogate-safety measures fall out directly: PET, the time between one user leaving a point and the next arriving at it, and TTC, the time to collision if both hold their present velocity. Car-following is excluded — only genuinely crossing paths count.

Object-level insight

per road user

Every road user carries a record: what it is, how big it actually is, what colour it is, how it accelerated, where it went, and what it nearly hit. Dimensions are measured, not looked up — the detector returns an image-axis box, but the heading is known from the trajectory, so the projection inverts to give true length and width.

Map-native output

bound to the road network

The SRT telemetry gives the ground-plane projection; the drone’s own GPS fix gives the origin. Every trajectory is then bound to a real OpenStreetMap link, a direction along it, and a lane — moment to moment. Results stop being a floating point cloud and become statements about a named piece of road.

Real OSM link geometry. Desire lines follow the median observed path for each movement, so they trace the road rather than cutting across it; width is proportional to volume. Queue extents are drawn along the carriageway they occur on.

Congestion across the whole recording

detector-free

The tracked window is 120 s, but the recording is 399 s. This covers all of it using frame statistics alone. Separating motion from occupancy distinguishes the three states a single signal confuses — and the one that matters, a standing queue, is present but not moving.

Behaviour outside the vocabulary

open-set

Fielded systems detect a fixed list and are blind to everything else. Instead of enumerating event types, this learns what normal looks like per location — the prevailing heading and speed in each cell of the scene — and reports departures from it. Nothing below was configured in advance.
